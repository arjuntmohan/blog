*Open with a moment, not a thesis. What happened recently that made you want to start writing
about this? A conversation, something you noticed at a job or in class, a headline that bugged you.*
(Observing)

## The question I can't stop thinking about

*State your big question plainly. Why does it matter to you specifically, and to people like you?*
(Questioning)

## What I think so far

*Lay out your current understanding, even if it's half-formed. What connection are you seeing that
other people might be missing? Could something from a totally different field explain what's
happening in tech?* (Associating)

## What I'm going to do about it

*What will you explore here over the next few months? People you want to talk to, tools you
want to try, experiments you want to run.* (Networking, Experimenting)

## Your turn

*End with a real question for readers, something they can answer in one comment.
For example: "What's one thing you're doing right now to prepare for an AI-shaped career, or are
you ignoring it entirely?"*
